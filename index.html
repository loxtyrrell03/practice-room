Practice Room

Every day to the recital, planned. Your journal, your programme, and a coach that knows the whole score.

Connect

Everything lives in your private GitHub repo (practice-room-data). Paste an access token once — it stays in this browser only.

Advanced

Getting the token: open any terminal and run gh auth token — paste what it prints. (Prefer a scoped token? A fine-grained PAT with Contents read/write on practice-room-data works too — see the repo README.)

Day 1
28 days to curtain

Morning cold test

10 min · before warm-up

First playing of the day, from memory, no warm-up. Tap each piece you tested.

tap once = ✓ passed · twice = ✕ broke down · three times = clear

End of day

One slow pass (50–60%) of everything you played fast. Then tell the coach how it went — it writes your journal and builds tomorrow.
